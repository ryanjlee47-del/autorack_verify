Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of publication]

Autorack, LLC (“Autorack”, “we”, “us”), 2300 Hastings Drive, Belmont, CA 94002, provides barcode pick-verification software for warehouses (the “Service”). This policy explains what information the Service handles, why, and the choices you have. Businesses that use Autorack (“Customers”) sign our Application License Agreement, which also governs how we treat their data.

Information we collect

Account and contract information

  • Names, work email addresses and roles of the people who use the dashboard.
  • Company name and address, and the details of the license agreement signature: signer name and title, time, IP address and browser, kept as a record of the contract.
  • Billing status. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers.

Warehouse data our Customers put into the Service

  • Orders and pick lists (order numbers, product barcodes, SKUs, descriptions, bin locations, quantities, and customer names if included).
  • Scan records: what was scanned, when, on which phone, by which worker, and the result.
  • Problem reports and photos workers take of them, and shipping tracking numbers.

Worker information

  • The name a Customer enters for each worker, and their PIN, which we store only as a one-way hash.
  • For linked phones: a label, the browser's user-agent string, and when it was last used. We do not collect location, contacts or other data from phones.

Technical and usage information

  • Server logs, including IP addresses, kept for security and troubleshooting.
  • Counts of which features are used (for example “pick sheets printed: 12”). These counts never include order or scan contents.

How we use information

  • To provide the Service: verifying picks, showing dashboards and reports, and sending the emails you choose (sign-in links, daily summaries, alerts, account and billing notices).
  • To secure the Service, prevent abuse (for example limiting PIN guesses) and fix problems.
  • To improve the Service using aggregated and anonymized statistics, as described in Section 5.3 of the License Agreement.
  • To keep records required by our contracts and the law.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only service providers who help us run Autorack, under contracts that limit their use of it:

  • Hosting and databases: [Render (application hosting), Neon (database)].
  • Email delivery: [Resend].
  • Payments: Stripe.

A warehouse can also share an order's proof of shipment with its own customer through a private link. That page shows the order, its scans and times, lot/serial numbers and tracking number, but not which worker picked it. The warehouse can turn the link off at any time. Stores a warehouse connects (such as Shopify or ShipStation) receive the tracking numbers of their own orders.

We may also disclose information when required by law or valid legal process, or to protect someone's safety.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The dashboard keeps your sign-in in your browser's local storage, and the scanner app stores orders and scans on the phone so it can work offline until they sync.

How long we keep it

Warehouse data is kept while the Customer's account is active, so scan history remains available as proof of shipments. When an account is closed, its data is permanently deleted 45 days later; owners can download a full copy from Settings until then (License Agreement, Section 9.1). Signature records are kept as long as needed to evidence the contract. [Confirm retention periods with counsel.]

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. PINs and sign-in tokens are stored hashed, sign-in is by single-use emailed links (no passwords), and scan history and audit logs can't be edited after the fact. No system is perfectly secure; please tell us at once if you suspect a problem.

Your choices and rights

Dashboard users can change their email preferences in Settings, and owners can download all of their warehouse's data or close the account there. Depending on where you live (for example under the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to know, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and not to be discriminated against for asking. Contact us at the address below.

If you are a worker at one of our Customers, your employer controls the warehouse data about you and we process it on their behalf. Please direct requests to your employer first; we will help them respond.

Children

Autorack is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the date above. If a change is significant, we'll also email account owners.
