Getting started

What should I do first?

Sign in to the dashboard and follow the setup checklist on the home page. The quickest way to see Autorack work: click Load sample orders, print the sample barcode sheet, link a phone and scan a few items. It takes about five minutes. When you're ready, delete the samples and import your real orders.

Who can do what?

Owners can do everything, including billing. Managers handle orders, workers and phones. Supervisors watch the floor and resolve problems, but can't change settings. Invite people from Team → Workers. They sign in with their Google account.

Warehouse workers don't need a Google account: they sign in on the phone with a 4-digit PIN.

We don't use Gmail. Can we still sign in with Google?

Yes. Any email address can be made into a Google account without creating a Gmail inbox: go to accounts.google.com/signup and choose Use your existing email.

Phones and scanning

How do I link a phone?

In the dashboard, open Team → Phones. Point the phone's camera at the QR code and tap the link: the scanner opens and links itself. Add it to the home screen when asked. You can also print a setup poster for the break room.

No camera handy? Open the scanner address shown on that page and type the setup code.

Which phones and scanners work?

Any recent iPhone or Android phone with a browser. Nothing to install from an app store. Bluetooth and USB barcode scanners that act as a keyboard work too, on phones and on the desktop pack station.

What happens when the Wi-Fi drops?

Keep scanning. The phone checks each scan itself and queues it, showing how many are waiting. When the connection comes back everything syncs, and the server checks every scan again.

How do new workers learn to scan?

On the phone, tap Practice scanning. Autorack makes a practice order from products on your own shelves. The worker finds and scans them exactly like a real order, sees green and red, and gets a score at the end. Nothing they scan counts, ships or changes stock. Managers see each worker's practice rounds on the Workers page.

A worker forgot their PIN.

Open Team → Workers, find the worker and choose Reset PIN. The new PIN is shown once; give it to them in person.

A phone was lost or stolen.

Open Team → Phones and unlink it. It stops working at once. If you think someone else has the setup code, choose Change setup code too; phones that are already linked keep working.

The phone camera is slow to scan, or scans the wrong thing.

Only the barcode inside the blue box counts, so barcodes on neighbouring bins are ignored. Hold the phone about a hand's width away, fill most of the box with the one barcode you want, and keep it still for a moment. To scan another unit of the same item, take the first one out of the box, then bring in the next. In dim aisles tap 💡 for the light. Shrink-wrapped or shiny labels read better tilted slightly to cut the glare. If a worker scans all day, a Bluetooth scanner is faster still.

A barcode scans as wrong, but it's the right item.

Common format differences (leading zeros, UPC vs EAN) are handled automatically. When a vendor label really is a different code, open the order in the dashboard and teach Autorack that barcode once. It counts as that item from then on. You can also add extra barcodes to a product in the catalog.

Orders and imports

What should my CSV look like?

One row per order line, with columns for order_number, barcode and quantity. description, customer, sku and location are optional. Common headings like “Order #”, “UPC”, “Qty” and “Bin” are recognised. You'll see a preview before anything is created, and importing the same file twice never creates orders twice.

Can orders come in automatically?

Yes. Under Setup → Connections you can connect Shopify, WooCommerce or ShipStation, pull from a Google Sheet or a web address, or email CSV files to your warehouse's import address.

The same mistakes keep happening. What do I fix?

Open Results → Insights and look at Mixed up most often. It lists the pairs of products that keep getting swapped, where each one sits, and the likeliest fix: two look-alikes in neighbouring bins, two items sharing a bin, or a barcode that isn't in your catalog yet. Fixing the top pair usually removes the most mistakes.

Can I show my clients how accurate we are?

Yes. Upload your logo in Settings, then open a client under Setup → Clients and download their accuracy report: orders shipped, how often items were picked right the first time, wrong items caught before shipping, and on-time rate, under your logo. It's emailed to the client's portal logins on the 1st of each month (you can turn that off per client), and they can download it from their portal any time.

A worker flagged a problem. What now?

The order waits on the dashboard with the worker's note and photos. A manager or supervisor can resolve it, ship it short, or send it back to be picked.

Billing

How much does it cost?

$290 a year or $29 a month per warehouse, flat. Unlimited workers, phones and scans. The 14-day trial doesn't need a card, and subscribing early keeps the rest of your trial free.

What is the founding customer price?

Warehouses that sign up during our founding period keep the price they signed up at for as long as they stay subscribed, even when prices go up for new customers. You'll see a Founding customer badge on your Billing page. If you cancel and the subscription ends, the founding price ends with it.

Can I switch between yearly and monthly?

Yes. Open Billing → Manage billing and choose Update plan. Yearly is two months free.

How do I cancel?

Open Billing → Manage billing. Your subscription runs to the end of the period you've paid for. Afterwards scanning pauses, but your orders, scans and reports stay available to view and export.

A payment failed.

Scanning keeps working for a grace period while you update your card in Billing → Manage billing. Stripe retries automatically once the card is updated.

Your data

Can I get my data out?

Any time. Results → Insights exports scans and orders as CSV, and Settings has a one-click download of everything as a ZIP.

How do I close my account?

An owner can close the account from Settings. Billing is cancelled and scanning stops at once. Your data is deleted 45 days later, with a reminder first, and you can reopen any time before then.
