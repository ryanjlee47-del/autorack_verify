FAQ

Questions

Do we have to change our WMS?
No. Autorack sits alongside whatever you use. Orders come in by CSV (or typed in by hand for one-offs), and nothing is written back to your system.
What if the Wi-Fi drops mid-pick?
Keep scanning. The phone checks each scan itself and queues it, showing how many are waiting. When the connection returns everything syncs, and the server re-checks every scan.
What phones work?
Any recent iPhone or Android with a browser. Nothing to install from an app store: it adds itself to the home screen. Bluetooth "keyboard" barcode scanners work too.
Our barcodes don't match our pick list exactly.
Common format differences are handled automatically. For vendor labels that are truly different codes, teach Autorack once from the dashboard and that barcode counts as the item from then on.
How do workers sign in?
Each worker gets a 4-digit PIN. Phones are linked to your warehouse once by scanning a setup QR code. You can unlink a lost phone from the dashboard.
What happens if we stop paying?
Scanning pauses; your data stays available to view and export. Scans already made on phones still sync.
