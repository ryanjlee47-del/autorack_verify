Trust

Security at Autorack

Last updated: [date of publication]

Autorack holds your orders, your scan history and information about your team. This page describes, in plain terms, how we protect it. It reflects how the software is actually built; if something here stops being true, we'll update the page.

Where your data lives

  • The application runs on [Render] and the database on [Neon Postgres], both in the [United States].
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) everywhere, and [encrypted at rest by our database provider].
  • The database is backed up continuously with point-in-time recovery [retention per plan: confirm].
  • Card payments are handled entirely by Stripe. Card numbers never touch our servers.
  • API keys for connected stores (Shopify, ShipStation, WooCommerce) are stored encrypted, are never shown again after you save them, and are erased when you disconnect the store.

Signing in

  • No passwords. Managers sign in with a single-use link emailed to them, valid for 15 minutes. There's no password to be guessed, reused or phished.
  • Sign-in links and sessions are stored only as one-way hashes, so a copy of our database wouldn't let anyone sign in. Sessions can be revoked at any time.
  • Workers sign in on linked phones with a 4-digit PIN. PINs are stored only as salted hashes (PBKDF2), and a phone locks after 5 wrong tries (25 across a warehouse) for 10 minutes.
  • A phone only works after it's been linked with your warehouse's setup code, and you can unlink a lost phone from the dashboard instantly.

Keeping customers apart

  • Every record belongs to exactly one warehouse, and every query is scoped to the signed-in warehouse.
  • An automated test runs on every change and tries every endpoint with another customer's IDs, failing the release if anything leaks.
  • Roles limit what each person can do: owners, managers, and supervisors who can't see billing or change orders.

A record you can trust

  • Scan history, the activity log and signed agreements are append-only: the database itself refuses edits, so no bug or person can quietly rewrite what happened.
  • Every change by your team, your phones and our staff is in your warehouse's activity log.
  • Autorack staff access to customer data is limited to a few named people. Any change they make to your account, and any export of your data, is recorded in your warehouse's activity log.

In the browser and on the phone

  • A strict Content Security Policy blocks injected scripts, and pages can't be framed by other sites.
  • Exports protect against spreadsheet formula injection.
  • Phones keep orders and unsynced scans only in the app's own storage, and only for the warehouse they're linked to.

Your data, your control

  • Owners can download everything (orders, scans, photos, workers, the activity log and the signed agreement) at any time from Settings.
  • When an account is closed, its data is permanently deleted after 45 days, with a reminder beforehand. We can delete sooner on request.

If something goes wrong

We follow a written incident-response process.
