How it works

1. Upload today's manifest

CSV, TSV, an Excel export, or just paste a list of barcodes. We handle the column-matching.

2. Print the door QR

One QR per shift. Workers scan it once at the door, enter their name, and the shift downloads to their phone.

3. Scan and go

Camera or Bluetooth ring scanner. Right item: a beep and a green flash. Wrong item: a hard stop before it ships.

Built for the way barcodes actually behave

Different scanners, same product

UPC-E, UPC-A, EAN-13, GS1-128 with lot and serial numbers -- we normalize all of them to the same product before comparing.

Never guesses on a maybe

If a code could match more than one line on the manifest, we never bill for it. Ambiguous scans go to your review queue, not your invoice.

Works with the wifi you have

A full shift downloads once at the door. From there, phones need no network at all -- not even a flaky one.
